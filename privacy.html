YOUR DATA

Privacy policy.

How Geometry Defense handles gameplay, purchases, support and visits to this website.

Effective date: 21 October 2026

At a glance

  • Gameplay progress is saved on your device.
  • There is no Geometry Defense account, game backend or cloud-save service.
  • The game includes no advertising or third-party tracking or analytics SDKs.
  • Apple processes optional in-app purchases. The app keeps local purchase records to deliver Diamonds and prevent duplicate grants.
  • If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send.
  • This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub processes website requests under its own privacy policy.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Geometry Defense iPhone game, this supporting website and communications with the game’s developer. For questions or privacy requests, contact tony-dev86@outlook.com.

Gameplay information stored on your device

The game stores currencies, upgrades, tier and wave records, statistics, settings, equipped and owned items, boosters, research, challenges and saved-run checkpoints in local app files, including a backup save. These records support gameplay, progression, resuming a run and purchase delivery.

They are not automatically sent to a developer-operated server. Your device’s backup and restore services may separately handle app data according to your Apple settings. Geometry Defense does not provide its own cloud synchronisation or cross-device recovery.

Apple purchases and local purchase records

Optional Diamond packs are processed through Apple StoreKit. Apple handles the Apple Account authentication, payment details and purchase authorisation. The game does not receive your payment-card details, bank details or Apple Account password.

The app receives purchase results from Apple and keeps transaction and product identifiers, purchase dates, environment, Diamond delivery records and relevant refund or revocation information locally. These records are used to verify delivery, avoid duplicate grants and handle unfinished transactions and refunds. Full signed transaction payloads are not retained in the game’s JSON save.

A random purchase identifier is stored in the iOS Keychain and supplied to Apple as an app account token to correlate purchases. It is not an advertising identifier, game account or recovery service. Keychain data can persist after the app is removed; deleting the app should not be assumed to erase that identifier.

Apple processes commerce information under its own terms. See Apple’s App Store privacy information and Apple’s privacy policy.

Support and diagnostics

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any details or attachments you choose to include. We use them to respond, investigate problems and maintain support records. Your email provider and ours process those communications.

Please remove personal information from screenshots and logs. Do not send passwords, card details or full signed transaction payloads. We do not require these to provide ordinary support.

Apple may make diagnostic or crash information available to the developer through App Store Connect or TestFlight, subject to Apple’s settings and policies. The game does not add a separate third-party crash-reporting or telemetry service.

Website hosting and external links

These pages contain no advertising, analytics scripts, embedded social widgets or tracking pixels. They do not set application cookies or use browser storage. Fonts and images are served with the site.

GitHub hosts this website through GitHub Pages and may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, browser information and request times, to deliver, secure and operate its services. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following links to Apple or another website brings you under that service’s policies.

Use, sharing and retention

We use information you provide to deliver requested support, investigate faults and meet applicable obligations. We do not sell your personal information or use it for cross-app advertising. Service providers used to host these pages or deliver support messages handle information for those functions. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect rights and security.

Local game records remain on your device until they are changed or removed through the app or device’s normal operation. Support communications are retained for as long as reasonably needed to resolve and follow up on the issue and meet applicable record-keeping obligations. Apple and GitHub determine retention for information they independently process.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can play without creating an account and can choose not to make purchases or contact support. Device settings control Apple services, backups and relevant diagnostics preferences.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information we hold, or to raise a concern with your privacy regulator. Email tony-dev86@outlook.com to make a request. We may need limited information to identify the support records concerned. We cannot remotely access or recover a device-only save, or erase records independently held by Apple or GitHub.

There is no game account to delete. Removing the app may remove its local game files and cause permanent loss of progress or unspent Diamonds. It does not necessarily remove Keychain information, device backups or Apple’s purchase records. Contact support before removing the app if you are investigating missing currency or a purchase.

Children

The game does not ask players to register an account or provide personal information during gameplay. Apple’s device and family controls can restrict purchases. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support that should be removed, contact us.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy if the game’s data handling changes and revise the effective date above. Contact the Geometry Defense developer at tony-dev86@outlook.com with questions about this policy.
