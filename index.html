AT YOUR PACE

Play combat offline, switch between 1×, 2× and 3× speed, and resume a living run from its latest saved checkpoint when you return.

Diamonds

Your extra options

Tap glowing pickups during play, or buy optional packs through Apple. Spend them on currency, timed boosters, tower skins and battlefield backgrounds.

Equip a skin and a background to change your look and add their listed stat bonuses. Optional Diamond purchases are consumable in-app purchases; pricing and availability are shown in the app.
