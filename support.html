iCloud Save: back up and restore

iCloud Save is available on supported iPhone versions. It keeps a complete backup in your private iCloud account, including permanent upgrades, Energy, Diamonds, research, unlocked items, settings, statistics and any resumable run. Normal play and local saving continue offline.

Back up before reinstalling or changing iPhones

  1. Sign in to iCloud in iPhone Settings and connect to the internet.
  2. In Geometry Defense, open Settings → iCloud Save and tap Back Up Now.
  3. Wait for the successful backup message and check its timestamp. Keep the app installed until you have confirmed that backup.

Restore on an iPhone

  1. Use the same iCloud account and the same game environment as the backup.
  2. Open Settings → iCloud Save → Refresh iCloud.
  3. Compare the device and iCloud progress summaries, tap Restore Backup and confirm. This replaces your entire local profile, including currencies and the saved run; the two profiles are not combined.
  4. The game returns to the main menu. Choose Resume Run if the restored backup contains an unfinished run.

Automatic backup periodically uploads saved changes when iCloud is available. A local checkpoint can be newer than the cloud backup, and closing or backgrounding the app does not guarantee that an upload finished. The last successful backup shown in the game is the recovery point.

If two devices have different progress, review both summaries before choosing which complete profile to keep. Changing iCloud accounts does not make the previous account’s backup available. This feature keeps one current backup per game/test environment, without a selectable history of older backups. Local Xcode testing, TestFlight and App Store progress are kept separate.

You can switch Automatic backup off and still use manual backup and restore. Switching it off does not delete an existing backup. See how iCloud backup data is handled.

Common questions

How do Gold, Energy and Diamonds work?

Gold buys temporary upgrades during a run and resets when you start a new one. Energy buys permanent base upgrades and research. Collect Diamonds by tapping their glowing pickups, or buy optional fixed Diamond packs through Apple. Spend Diamonds on currency packs, boosters, skins and backgrounds. Gold packs require an active run.

How do I resume an unfinished run?

Reopen the game and choose Resume Run when a valid checkpoint is available. The game saves periodically and on pause or backgrounding, so an unexpected shutdown may return you to the latest successful checkpoint. Starting a new run replaces the previous unfinished run.

A Diamond purchase is missing. What should I do?

Keep the app installed and reconnect to the internet. Open Diamond Shop → Buy Diamonds → Check purchases to reconcile unfinished Apple transactions. Avoid buying the same pack again while investigating. If the balance is still wrong, contact support with the pack name, approximate time and error message.

Check purchases does not rebuild a lost Diamond balance from already-finished consumable purchases. Progress and the wallet are stored together locally. In builds with iCloud Save available, a successful whole-profile backup can also recover the balance and delivery history from that backup; it does not reconstruct later purchases or merge balances.

Why is a Diamond pack unavailable?

Apple must return the package and its localised price before it can be purchased. Check your connection, tap Retry store and try again later. If the issue continues, email the exact status sentence above the packages and the app version/build. Apple Account purchase restrictions may also affect checkout.

Are TestFlight purchases real purchases?

TestFlight uses Apple’s sandbox and does not charge real money. TestFlight and local Xcode StoreKit testing use separate test saves. Test balances are not a promise of transferable App Store currency.

Can I back up or move my progress with iCloud?

Use Settings → iCloud Save on a supported iPhone version. Back Up Now saves the complete profile; Restore Backup replaces the profile on the receiving device after confirmation. Both devices must use the same iCloud account and game environment. Follow the backup and restore steps above and confirm a successful backup before removing the app.

iCloud Save is unavailable or my backup did not finish.

Check that your iPhone is signed in to iCloud, has a working connection and has available iCloud storage. Use an up-to-date game version, then open Settings → iCloud Save and tap Refresh iCloud. If it remains unavailable, keep the app installed and email support with the exact status message, app version and iOS version. Local gameplay and saving remain available while iCloud cannot connect.

How do I request a refund?

Apple manages App Store purchase refunds. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and follow Apple’s instructions. Geometry Defense cannot approve an Apple refund. A refund can remove remaining Diamonds; if those Diamonds were already spent, later grants may repay the outstanding amount before further Diamond spending is available.

The game feels slow or effects are distracting.

Open Settings and lower visual quality or choose Low performance mode. You can also adjust sound, vibration, screen shake and damage numbers. Pause and resume or restart the app if needed; keep it installed while investigating save or purchase issues.

Can I play without a connection?

Normal combat, waves, local progression and earned-currency upgrades work offline. Apple product information, purchases, purchase reconciliation and iCloud backup/restore require the relevant Apple services and a connection. Offline changes stay local until a backup succeeds.

Before you reinstall

Your progress, unspent Diamonds and purchase-delivery history are stored on this device. Before removing the app, follow the iCloud backup steps and confirm a recent successful backup. Without a matching backup, reinstalling or deleting the save can lose that progress.

Contact support first if a purchase or save seems wrong.
